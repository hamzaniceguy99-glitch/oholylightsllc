How we work

A Florida company, and not electricians

O Holy Lights LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, document number L23000462929, filed in 2023, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

We connect lighting to existing, suitable exterior outlets. We are not licensed electrical contractors and we install no circuits, outlets or permanent wiring — if a property needs another GFCI-protected exterior outlet, that goes to a licensed electrician before we start. We use clips made for the purpose rather than nails or staples into fascia and gutters, we do not work at height in high winds, and we will decline an installation where a roof or gutter cannot safely take it.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Is takedown really included?

Yes, and the date is set when we install. A company that charges separately for removal in January is relying on you having forgotten to ask in October.

Will you damage my gutters or roof?

Not with nails or staples — we use clips designed for the purpose. If gutters or fascia are too fragile to carry a run safely, we will tell you rather than fixing to them anyway.

Can you add an outlet for the display?

No. We are not licensed electricians. If you need another exterior GFCI outlet, a licensed electrical contractor needs to install it first, and we will say so at the design visit.

What if a section goes out mid-December?

Tell us and we will come and replace it. In-season maintenance is part of the package, not a call-out charge.

Do I own the lights?

That depends on the package and is stated in the quote. Either way we label and store everything off-season so next year is faster to install.

When should I book?

Earlier than feels necessary. Installation schedules fill through the autumn, and the last two weeks before the season are usually full.

Thinking about this year?

A free design visit and a quote that includes taking them down again.
